Natural Spawns
=================

**Requires [AdditionalSpawnContraints](https://mods.vintagestory.at/additionalspawnconstraints)**

Overview
--------

Only allows spawns of drifters above natural blocks, even during storms; blocks are considered natural if their name satisfies any of the filters below:

 - `"game:sand-*"`
 - `"game:gravel-*"`
 - `"game:dirtygravel"`
 - `"game:sludgygravel"`
 - `"game:muddygravel"`
 - `"game:soil-*"`
 - `"game:forestfloor-*"`
 - `"game:peat-*"`
 - `"game:rock-*"`
 - `"game:crackedrock-*"`
 - `"game:regolith-*"`
 - `"game:ore-*"`
 - `"game:saltpeter-*"`
 - `"game:looseboulders-*"`
 - `"game:loosestones-*"`
 - `"game:looseores-*`

Might adversely affect dungeon spawns, but keeps helps keep drifters out of your builds even if they are unlit.
